# Google Flow production handoff

The validated IMAGE sheet is a deterministic composite of independently generated and validated camera panels.

Clip 1 interprets that composite sequentially:
PANEL 1 → SHOT 1 → hard CUT → PANEL 2 → SHOT 2

The reference sheet is never shown onscreen. Veo/Google Flow handles temporal motion and camera cuts; image generation supplies clean visual anchors.

Clip 2 is text-only Extend from the exact final visual/audio state of Clip 1.

Two panels are preferred for a simple ~8-second interaction. Maximum 4.
